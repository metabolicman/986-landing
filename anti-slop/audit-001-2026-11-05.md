# Anti-slop audit 001 (2026-11-05)

Page: https://buy-986-app.netlify.app (main at the time of audit)
Scope: small refresh. Copy tweaks, imagery, decoration, motion, contrast. Checkout, pricing logic, links and meta tags were not touched.

## Read as generic or AI-made (fixed in this branch)

- Pill badge above the H1 ("One-time purchase. Lifetime access."). Removed. The same facts already sit in the button and proof line.
- Uppercase, wide-tracked labels on every section. Now sentence case at 14px.
- Emoji icons on persona and feature cards, and check/cross emoji in the comparison table. Removed. Table cells now say "Yes, ..." or "No, ...".
- 01/02/03 step numbers in "How it works". Replaced with a thin seafoam rule over each step.
- 7 identical feature cards with one orphan on desktop. The privacy card now spans the full row.
- Gradient stripe on the pricing card, glow shadows and scale-on-hover on buttons. Removed.
- Hover lift on cards that are not links. Removed.
- Count-up number animation. Removed.
- Stock "sad person" persona photos, and an infrared forehead thermometer that does not match the app's oral or armpit method. Replaced with quiet still-life photos that show no faces and no app screens.
- Unused before/after images with fake temperature chips (hero-lifestyle, warmth-narrative, hero-coastal). Deleted.
- Copy tells: "not X, it's Y" lines, slogan-style headings, three-word stacks, "only app", "none of them", "in two minutes", "within a week". Rewritten in plain words (see PR for before/after).
- Duplicate science trust paragraphs. Merged.
